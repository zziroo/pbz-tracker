I wanted to play blind. I also didn't want to reach the end and find out I'd locked myself out of the true ending because of some side quest I never heard of. And reading a full missables guide kind of defeats the point of going in blind.

So I made a small tool, pbz-tracker. It's not a guide, more like a seatbelt. A guide tells you what's coming. This only tells you where to stop.

It reads your save (read-only), checks it against the known missables and warns you before a point of no return if a quest or item is still open. By default you only see the warnings that matter right now. If nothing is at risk it says "No risk of missing anything right now." and you go back to the game.

The spoiler part was the whole point. Warnings only say WHERE to stop, like "before this area's final fight" or "before the story moves you to the next major area". You won't see boss names, future regions or story events in there. A quest name only shows up if that quest is already in your journal, and anything past where you are is never shown. The Journal tab (quest status, step counters, missed items) is blurred. A row only opens when you click it, so if you're playing blind, don't click it.

It runs in your browser: https://zziroo.github.io/pbz-tracker/
Click the link, choose your game folder, that's it. Nothing is downloaded and no file is uploaded.

The practical stuff:
- Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- Tabs: Warnings (default), Journal (blurred), Settings, About.
- Leave it open next to the game. It refreshes by itself every time the game saves (resting at a bell, autosave).
- Folder access needs Chrome or Edge. In Firefox/Safari you can drag & drop your save (and PBZ.exe once), but there's no auto refresh.
- It all runs in the browser. The key is derived from your own PBZ.exe and stays in that browser.
- Read-only. It never writes to your save, backups or game folder, and it doesn't touch the running game.
- The UI is in English and Turkish (TR/EN switch). If you have a community text patch installed, quest names come from it automatically.
- It shows the game build it was tested on (26367565) and warns you if yours is newer.
- If you'd rather have a local app, there's a Windows exe too. Double-click it and a window opens. Local only, no internet. It's unsigned, so SmartScreen will complain (More info, then Run anyway). The source is public if you'd rather build it yourself.
- Rule fixes: the web version updates itself. For the exe, drop the repo's latest data/rules.json next to it.

To be honest, this is v0.5.0 and I built it fast. Some later story cutoffs can't be detected from the save yet, so warnings there go by quest state only. A few quest-ID mappings are educated guesses (marked with *). A few items can't be read from the save at all. And the rules come from guides and player reports, which don't always agree with each other. So it's not perfect. If it flags something wrong or misses something, tell me and I'll fix it.

The rules come from public guides (Game8, PowerPyx, DTGRE, the Steam Community guides at https://steamcommunity.com/app/4115450/guides/ ), this forum's point-of-no-return thread (https://steamcommunity.com/app/4115450/discussions/0/572679490124259489/ ) and player reports from the Steam forums. All credited in the repo. Thanks to everyone who posted what they found.
